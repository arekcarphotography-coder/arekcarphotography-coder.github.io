How to prepare your car for a photoshoot — 7 simple steps

17 November 2026 · Arek Carphotography

A good shoot starts long before the shutter is pressed. A few simple things on your side make the photos noticeably better and save us time on location.

1. Wash the car the day before

High-resolution photos show every water spot and speck of dust. Wash the car the day before and just wipe it down with a quick detailer on location.

2. Wheels, tyres and glass

Clean wheels and dressed (but not mirror-shiny) tyres make a huge difference. Clean the windows inside and out — dirty reflections show up surprisingly strongly.

3. Empty the interior

Phone mounts, chargers, air fresheners, things in the door pockets — all of it shows up in interior shots and through the windows.

4. Pick the time of day

The best light is usually early morning and just before sunset. An overcast day can be great too — soft light shows off the body lines beautifully.

5. A location that suits the car

A classic looks best in the mountains or an old town, a modern supercar in an industrial or minimalist setting. We choose the location together before the shoot.

6. Plan your time

A standard shoot usually takes 1–3 hours, depending on the number of locations and rolling shots. Have a full tank and some spare time.
