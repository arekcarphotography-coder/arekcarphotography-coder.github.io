A weekend on Lake Como — Koenigsegg CC850, Sián and Zonda Cervino

24 May 2026 · Arek Carphotography

In May the whole car world heads to Lake Como — several events take place over a single weekend, and you can find some of the rarest cars on the planet on almost every corner. Here's my report from the trip.

Day 0 — through the Alps

The trip started with a drive through alpine villages. Mist on the passes, fuel stops and the first supercars heading the same way we were.

Como — day 0: the alpine drive

Koenigsegg CC850

A white CC850 in a rainy villa garden. The wet ground and soft light turned out to be allies — the white paint and the signature wheels looked great in those conditions.

Koenigsegg CC850

Lamborghini Sián and Pagani Zonda Cervino

Two completely different characters: the light-blue Sián with copper wheels and the silver-blue Zonda Cervino among the lakeside greenery. Both got galleries of their own.

Pagani Zonda Cervino

Kimera EVO38 in Martini colours

And finally something for rally fans — the Kimera EVO38 in Martini Racing colours, photographed from every angle, engine bay included.
