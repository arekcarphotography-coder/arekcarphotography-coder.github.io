The ICE St. Moritz 2026 — a concours on ice, reported

8 February 2026 · Arek Carphotography

The ICE St. Moritz is one of Europe's most unusual motoring events — a concours d'elegance held on a frozen lake in the Swiss Alps. In 2026 I spent three days there with my camera. Here's a short report with the best shots.

Day 0 — St. Moritz before the event

Before the cars went out on the ice, the town itself was an exhibition. A garage full of Paganis, classics parked on snowy streets and the first runs on the lake — a perfect day for details and calm shots.

The ICE — day 0: St. Moritz

On the ice — panning

The hardest and most rewarding part. Panning means following a moving car with the camera at a slower shutter speed — the car stays sharp while the background blurs with motion. On white ice with a snowy forest behind, the effect is special, but every keeper comes with plenty of misses.

The ICE — on the ice

Paddock, people & atmosphere

The ICE isn't only about cars. It's the owners, mechanics, guests in fur coats and the whole setting that makes this event look like nowhere else. I tried to catch the moments in between — conversations by the cars, preparations and emotions.

The ICE — paddock, people & atmosphere

Stars of the trip

Two cars got galleries of their own: the McLaren F1 GTR in red ‘Lark’ livery and the Koenigseggs I photographed in the snow around St. Moritz.
