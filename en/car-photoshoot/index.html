Car photo shoot

I photograph cars from my base in Rzeszów — across south-eastern Poland and anywhere else in the country. Every shoot is planned around the car: the place, the time of day and the shots that best show its character.

Types of shoots

A shoot of your car

A location around Rzeszów or wherever suits the car — full shots, details, the interior and rolling shots.

Examples: Audi RS5 · Audi R8 · Audi RS3 MTM · Pagani Zonda HP Barchetta

Photos for a car sale

Shots that make a listing stand out: the body, the interior and the details shown as the car really looks.

Dealerships and detailing

Shoots of cars in stock and finished detailing work — in Rzeszów, south-eastern Poland and anywhere in the country.

Event coverage

Car meets, track days and concours — in Poland and across Europe.

Examples: The ICE St. Moritz 2026 · Supercar Owners Circle 2025 · Lake Como 2026

How a shoot works

  1. Get in touch Tell me about the car, what the photos are for and when you'd like the shoot — via the form or e-mail.
  2. Place and time We choose a location that suits the car and the time of day with the best light.
  3. The shoot Usually 1–3 hours: full shots, details, the interior and — if you like — rolling shots.
  4. Selection and editing I pick the best frames and edit each of them.
  5. Final photos We agree on the number of photos and the delivery date before the shoot.

Where I shoot

I'm based in Rzeszów — I shoot across south-eastern Poland and travel elsewhere in the country on request. For events I also travel abroad, e.g. to The ICE in St. Moritz and Supercar Owners Circle in Sardinia.

Pricing

Every shoot is quoted individually — the price depends on the type of shoot, the location and the scope. Tell me about the car and what the photos are for, and I'll reply with a quote and a proposed date.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you do car shoots?

I'm based in Rzeszów, so most shoots are in Rzeszów and south-eastern Poland. I also travel elsewhere in Poland, and abroad for events such as The ICE in St. Moritz or Supercar Owners Circle in Sardinia.

What cars do you photograph?

Any car with character — supercars and hypercars, classics, well-built tuning projects and everyday cars their owners simply love.

How long does a shoot take?

A standard shoot usually takes 1–3 hours, depending on the number of locations and whether we do rolling shots.

How much does a car shoot cost?

It depends on the type of shoot, the location and the scope. Tell me about the car and what the photos are for — I'll reply with a quote and a proposed date.

How should I prepare the car?

Above all a washed car, clean wheels and glass and an empty interior. I've described it step by step in a blog guide. Read the guide →

Do you work with businesses?

Yes — dealerships, used-car dealers, detailing studios and event organisers. Tell me what you need and I'll send a proposal.
