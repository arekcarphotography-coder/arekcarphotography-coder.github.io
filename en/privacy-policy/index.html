Privacy policy

This website is an automotive photographer's portfolio. Below I explain what data reaches me through the site and what happens to it.

Data controller

The data controller is Arek Carphotography, contact: arek.carphotography@gmail.com.

Contact form and e-mail

When you write via the form or by e-mail, I process the data you provide: name, e-mail address, optionally phone number, shoot date and location, and your message. I use it only to reply and prepare an offer.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps taken at your request before entering into a contract) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (handling correspondence).

I keep messages for as long as the conversation lasts and then for no longer than 12 months — unless we work together, in which case for as long as the law requires (e.g. tax rules).

Who receives the data

Messages go to my mailbox (Google — Gmail) and to the provider hosting the website and handling the form. I don't sell data or share it with anyone for marketing.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your data — just write to arek.carphotography@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych).

Cookies and browser storage

This site uses no cookies and no tracking tools. It only remembers your chosen language (in browser storage on your device). Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your device's IP address.

Photos

All photos on this site are my own work and protected by copyright. If you appear in one of them and would like it removed, just let me know.
